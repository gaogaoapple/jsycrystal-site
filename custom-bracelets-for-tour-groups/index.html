How to Order Custom Bracelets for a Tour Group: Timeline, Name Tags and Packaging

What to decide, what to send us and how far ahead to start, written from the orders we handle for travel companies, retreat hosts and group organizers.

What we handle for you

You deal with one partner. We coordinate beads, accessories, branding and packaging across our Shanwei partner workshops, check quality, and ship everything together.

  • Beads and stones — natural stone first; other bead materials on request
  • Charms and accessories
  • Tags and engraving — logo or name tags
  • Gift boxes, cards and packaging
  • Sample approval
  • Quality checks (QC)
  • Consolidated shipping

A natural stone bracelet is one of the few trip gifts people keep wearing after they get home. It is small, light to carry, and it can carry the group's name or the trip year on a tag. It also has to arrive on time, fit thirty different wrists and look like one set.

This guide walks through the decisions in the order we usually work through them with a tour operator. If you already know what you want, skip to the checklist or send us your brief.

1. Work backwards from the day you hand them out

Start with the date the bracelets need to be in your hands, not the departure date. Then count back:

  • Shipping: about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx; about 25–35 days by sea freight. For gift quantities, express is usual.
  • Bulk production: usually 15–30 days after you approve the sample, depending on quantity and how much is customized.
  • Sample and approval: we make one physical sample and send photos and video. Timing depends on the tag and box, and we tell you when we quote. Add the days your own team needs to sign off.

Put together, that is well over a month. Two months gives you room to change the tag wording or try a second stone. Groups that leave it to the last three weeks usually end up with a plain bracelet and no tag.

2. Choose one stone, or one stone with a variation

Most groups pick one stone for everyone. It photographs well as a set and keeps the order simple. Amethyst, rose quartz and citrine are the stones people ask for most as gifts. Tiger eye and obsidian suit groups with more men or a more understated style.

Some hosts pick one stone for the whole group and a different one for the guides or VIP guests. Mixed stones within one order are normal and do not change the minimum.

All our stones are natural. Colour and pattern vary a little from bead to bead, which is part of the point. We grade beads by colour and size before stringing so the group's bracelets look even next to each other.

3. Pick a bead size and sort out sizing

8 mm beads are the safe choice for a mixed group: clearly a gemstone bracelet, comfortable for most wrists. 6 mm is lighter and more delicate; 10 mm reads bolder and suits men's wrists. Our bead size guide has the details.

On elastic cord, one standard adult size fits most people. If you know your group, tell us roughly how many smaller and larger pieces you need. Lengths of about 15–20 cm can be mixed in one order. As a guide, an 8 mm bracelet for a standard adult wrist has around 22 beads.

4. Keep the tag text short

The tag is what turns a nice bracelet into this trip's bracelet. It is also small, so less is more:

  • Destination and year, for example "Kyoto 2027".
  • A group or company name.
  • A simple logo or symbol.

One or two short lines read well on a small tag. Long sentences belong on the printed card inside the box. Send us your wording or artwork and we review it before sampling; the sample shows you exactly how the tag will look. Tag shape and finish are confirmed on the sample too.

5. Decide how it will be presented

How the gift is handed over matters as much as the bracelet. The usual options:

  • Gift box with a printed card: the card can carry the itinerary name, a welcome line or a thank-you from the host.
  • Pouch: lighter and cheaper to ship, good for welcome gifts left in rooms.
  • Box plus card plus a name on each: works for small, high-value groups and incentive trips.

Printed cards and custom boxes can have their own minimums. We confirm these in the quote, so you are not surprised later.

6. Ask for the sample, and look at it properly

Do not skip the sample, even for a small group. Check the stone colour in daylight, the tag text and spelling, the fit on two or three different wrists and how the box closes. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project. Once you approve it, the sample becomes the standard for the whole order.

7. Plan delivery to one address

Most groups ship everything to one office address and pack the bracelets with other trip materials. Shipping to a hotel is possible, but someone has to receive and hold the box. Whichever you choose, allow a few days for customs clearance in the delivery country; your courier or customs broker can tell you what applies to gift goods there.

Checklist: what to send us for a quote

  • Number of guests, and how many smaller or larger sizes if you know
  • The date the gifts must be in your hands, and the delivery country
  • Stone preference, or the feeling you want (calm, bright, understated)
  • Tag wording or logo file
  • Packaging: box, pouch, printed card
  • Rough budget per gift, if you have one

Our minimum is 10 pieces per order, so small private groups are welcome. We reply within 24 hours.

Common questions

How far ahead should a tour operator order custom bracelets?

Add up the steps: sample time, your approval, 15–30 days of bulk production and 7–10 days of express shipping by DHL or FedEx. Start well over a month before the gifts are needed. Two months leaves room for a second sample or a change to the tag.

What is the minimum order for tour group bracelets?

Our minimum is 10 pieces per order, mixed freely across stones and sizes. Custom engraved tags, printed cards and boxes can have their own minimums, which we confirm in the quotation before you commit.

What should go on a tour group bracelet tag?

Keep it short: a destination and year, a group name, or a logo. A small tag reads best with one or two short lines. We review your wording or artwork and confirm it on the sample before production.

How do you size bracelets for a group with different wrists?

Most groups order one standard adult size on elastic cord, plus a few smaller and larger pieces. If you know the split, tell us how many of each length. Lengths of about 15–20 cm are possible within one order.

Can every bracelet in the group be the same?

Size, bead count, tag and box will match. The stones are natural, so colour and pattern vary slightly from bead to bead. We grade beads by colour and size before stringing so the set looks even.

Can you ship the gifts to our office or to the hotel?

We ship worldwide by DHL, FedEx or sea freight with tracking. Most groups ship to one office address and hand the gifts out on the trip. Allow time for customs at the delivery country.
