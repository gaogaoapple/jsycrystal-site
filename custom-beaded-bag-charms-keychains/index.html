What we make

Bag charms, keychains and phone charms in the same workshop flow

These pieces use the same natural stones and the same partner workshops as our wholesale crystal bracelets. The difference is how they are worn: clipped to a tote, a set of keys, a wristlet loop or a phone. Stainless steel clasps and rings are the usual hardware. Tassels and a small logo tag can be added when you want the piece to carry your shop or club name.

  • Beaded bag charms — a short strand or loop of natural stone for a tote, shopper or everyday bag, often with a tassel.
  • Beaded keychains — stone beads on a split ring, for shops, clubs and gift counters.
  • Wristlet keychains — a beaded loop that sits on the wrist so keys are harder to drop.
  • Phone charms and straps — a shorter beaded strand or strap that clips to a phone.
  • Tassels, logo tags and charms — silk or thread tassels, and a small tag or charm with your name or logo.

We also make crystal car charms, and beaded chains or straps for bags, phones, glasses and lanyards. If you want any of those in the same order, say so on the form and we will tell you what we can include.

Process

From idea to a packed shipment

  1. Send your idea or logo

    A rough sketch, a photo of a charm you like, stones and colours, a quantity, and your logo file if you have one, is enough to start.

  2. Mockup

    We reply within 24 hours with questions, a quotation and a mockup so you can see the layout of beads, tassel, tag and hardware.

  3. Sample approval

    When you want to hold the piece, we make a sample and send photos and video with it. Production starts after you approve it.

  4. Production

    Beads are graded by colour and size, then strung and finished to the approved sample. Bulk work usually takes 15–30 days, depending on quantity and how much is customized.

  5. QC

    We check counts, attachments, finish and packing, and can send inspection photos before dispatch.

  6. Consolidated shipping

    Charms, tags and boxes leave together. DHL or FedEx takes about 7–10 days; sea freight about 25–35 days for larger orders.

Care

A short note on water, sweat and metal parts

Natural stone should be kept away from sweat, pool water and long water exposure. Please do not treat a natural-stone charm as waterproof or as something to wear through a workout or a swim. Stainless steel clasps, rings and findings are waterproof and tarnish-free, so they are the usual choice for pieces that may see more daily wear. If a design uses gold-plated brass, those parts are waterproof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free.

FAQ

Bag charm and keychain questions

What is the minimum order for bag charms and keychains?

10 pieces per order in total. You can mix bag charms, keychains, wristlets, phone charms, stones and colours inside those 10 pieces. Branded extras such as engraved logo tags, printed cards or custom boxes can have their own minimums, and we confirm those in your quotation.

How much do custom bag charms and keychains cost?

Pricing depends on quantity, design and packaging, so we quote each inquiry. Tell us the stones, the product types, a rough quantity and whether you need logo tags or boxes, and the quotation will show every line clearly.

Can I mix bag charms, keychains and phone charms in one order?

Yes. Mixing is normal for a first order. Many shops and clubs start with a small spread of styles and stones, see what sells or gets used, then reorder the ones that work.

Can you add our logo or shop name to a bag charm or keychain?

Yes. Your logo or name can go on a small tag or charm on the piece, a hang tag, a printed card or a gift box. Artwork is reviewed with your mockup and sample before production. If you want a full private label range, see our private label page.

Are natural-stone bag charms waterproof?

Natural stone should be kept away from sweat, pool water and long water exposure. We do not describe natural stone as waterproof. Stainless steel clasps, rings and findings are waterproof and tarnish-free. If a design uses gold-plated brass, those parts are waterproof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free.

Can I order a sample before a bulk order?

Yes. After we agree a mockup, we can send a sample with photos and video. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project, and if any of the sample fee is credited against your bulk order, the quotation says so.

How long does a bag charm or keychain order take?

Bulk production usually runs 15–30 days after you approve the sample, depending on quantity and how much is customized. Shipping is added on top: about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or about 25–35 days by sea freight.

Do you also make car charms, phone straps and beaded chains?

Yes. Besides bag charms and keychains, we can make phone charms and straps, crystal car charms, and beaded chains or straps for bags, phones, glasses and lanyards. Tell us what you need in the same inquiry and we will say what we can include in the order.

Do I need separate suppliers for boxes, tags and charms?

No. We coordinate beads, charms, tags, boxes, cards and packaging across our partner workshops in Shanwei, run QC, and ship everything together. You deal with one team from quote to delivery.
