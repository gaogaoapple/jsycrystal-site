What we make

Bag straps, phone chains, glasses chains and lanyards

These pieces use the same natural stones and the same partner workshops as our wholesale crystal bracelets, beaded bag charms and keychains, and beaded phone charms and phone straps. What changes is the length and how they attach: a longer chain or strap for a bag, a phone, a pair of glasses, or to wear as a lanyard. Stainless steel clasps and rings are the usual hardware. If you have a particular bag fitting, case, glasses or clip in mind, send a photo with your inquiry and we will say what we can make.

  • Beaded bag straps — a longer strand of natural-stone beads that clips onto a bag as a strap or handle accent, often finished with a tassel or a logo tag. If the strap needs to carry the bag's weight, tell us how it will be used; we do not treat every beaded strap as a load-bearing shoulder strap.
  • Beaded phone chains — a longer beaded strand that clips to a phone. For short phone charms and wrist or short phone straps, see the phone charms and straps page.
  • Beaded glasses chains — a beaded chain that holds a pair of glasses around the neck, with ends agreed on the mockup and sample.
  • Beaded lanyards — a longer beaded strand worn around the neck for a badge, key or small charm, with length and attachment agreed before production.
  • Matching sets — a chain or strap boxed with a matching bag charm or keychain, a short phone charm, or a custom bracelet, for shops that sell sets at the counter.
  • Logo tags and cards — your shop or brand name on a small tag on the strand, a hang tag, or a printed card.

We also make crystal car charms and rearview mirror charms. Short bag charms, keychains and phone charms can go in the same order.

Before you order

Sweat, water and daily use

Glasses chains and lanyards rest on the neck. Phone chains sit in the hand. Bag straps rub against a bag and can get wet in the rain. That is a harder life than a bracelet kept in a box. Natural stone should be kept away from sweat, pool water and long water exposure. We do not describe natural stone as waterproof or sweat-proof. Shops can tell customers to keep the beads dry when they can, and to take the piece off before a workout or a swim.

Hooks, clasps, rings and other metal parts are stainless steel as standard, so they are waterproof and tarnish-free. If a design uses gold-plated brass for a warmer colour, those parts are rust-proof and waterproof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free.

Process

From idea to a packed shipment

  1. Send your idea

    Stones and colours, whether you want a bag strap, phone chain, glasses chain or lanyard, a photo of a piece, bag, case or glasses you like, a rough quantity and your logo file if you have one is enough to start.

  2. Mockup and quote

    We reply as soon as possible with questions, a quotation and a mockup showing beads, length, attachment, tassel, tag and packaging.

  3. Sample approval

    If a sample is needed, fees and shipping are quoted for your project. Production starts after you approve the specification.

  4. Production

    After you approve the specification, the pieces are strung and finished. Bulk work usually takes 15–30 days, depending on quantity and how much is customized.

  5. Packing

    Packing is arranged with the order.

  6. Consolidated shipping

    Chains, straps, tags and boxes leave together. DHL or FedEx takes about 7–10 days; sea freight about 25–35 days for larger orders.

What is the difference between a bag charm and a beaded bag strap, or a phone charm and a phone chain?

A bag charm is a shorter piece that hangs from a handle or zipper. A beaded bag strap is a longer strand meant to clip onto a bag as a strap or handle accent. A phone charm or short phone strap is a compact piece that clips to a phone; a phone chain is a longer beaded strand for the same kind of clip. Length and attachment are agreed on the mockup and sample. If you have a bag, case or pair of glasses in mind, send a photo with your inquiry. See also our bag charms and keychains and phone charms and straps pages.

Can I mix bag straps, glasses chains and lanyards in one order?

Yes. Mixing is normal for a first order. Many shops start with a small spread of chain and strap styles, plus bag charms or short phone charms, see what sells, then reorder the ones that work.

Can you add our logo or shop name?

Yes. Your logo or name can go on a small tag or charm on the strand, a hang tag, a printed card or a gift box. Artwork is reviewed with your mockup and sample before production.

No. Natural stone should be kept away from sweat, pool water and long water exposure. We do not describe natural stone as waterproof or sweat-proof. Stainless steel clasps, rings and findings are waterproof and tarnish-free.
