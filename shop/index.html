How ordering works

From a short message to a shipment

Send the styles you like, rough quantities and the packaging you need. We reply with options and a quotation, send samples with photos and video, and start bulk production after you approve them. Bulk production usually takes 15–30 days after sample approval, plus shipping. More on the how it works page.

Need other bead materials or crystal items? Tell us what you have in mind and we'll check what we can make or source. Natural stone bracelets stay our main line, and we'll tell you honestly if something isn't a good fit. Need it under your own brand?
