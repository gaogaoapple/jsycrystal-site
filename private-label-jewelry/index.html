Before You Start

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the bracelet design, materials, logo method and packaging. Send your estimated quantity so we can confirm a suitable option.

Can I order samples first?

Discuss a sample before bulk production. Sample fees, shipping costs and lead time are quoted for your project; any bulk-order credit must be agreed in your quotation.

Can I mix stones, sizes or designs?

Share the quantities you need for each style. We will confirm which combinations are feasible and any minimum quantities for each component.

What should I send for a quotation?

Send your brand or store website, a reference image or design idea, preferred stones, estimated quantity and delivery country. If specifications are undecided, tell us what you have in mind.

Can you make other bead materials or crystal items under my brand?

Need other bead materials or crystal items? Tell us what you have in mind and we'll check what we can make or source. Natural stone bracelets stay our main line, and we'll tell you honestly if something isn't a good fit.

Do I need separate suppliers for boxes, tags and beads?

No. We coordinate beads, charms, tags, boxes, cards and packaging across our partner workshops in Shanwei, run QC, and ship everything together. You deal with one team from quote to delivery.
