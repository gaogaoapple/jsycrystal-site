Order

How a private label order works

  1. Send request

    Shop type, the logo or tag you want, stones, a rough quantity, and the ship-to country.

  2. Design and sample

    We quote the bracelets and the branding. If a sample is needed, fees and shipping are quoted. The sample fee can be credited against the bulk order.

  3. Approve and bulk

    After you approve, JSY makes the order in Ketang.

  4. Packed and shipped

    Bracelets, tags and packaging ship together. Timing is confirmed with your order. See OEM manufacturing for the timelines stated there.

FAQ

Private label questions

What is the minimum for private label crystal bracelets?

10 finished bracelets per order, mixed across stones and styles. That minimum is for finished bracelets. For logo tags, cards and boxes, tell us the quantity and we will confirm.

Can a customer design be kept exclusive, and can you sign an NDA?

A customer design can be kept exclusive to that customer. We can sign an NDA.

Can the sample fee be credited against the bulk order?

If a sample is needed, fees and shipping are quoted for your project. The sample fee can be credited against the bulk order.

What can I brand, and do you provide images for listings?

Your logo can go on a hang tag or charm, a card, and a gift box or pouch. We provide resellers with a product image pack. Tell us the quantity for tags and boxes and we will confirm.

How long does a private label order take?

Timing is confirmed with your order. The timelines stated on our OEM page are the ones we use. We manufacture your bracelets in Ketang and ships the bracelets, tags and packaging together.
