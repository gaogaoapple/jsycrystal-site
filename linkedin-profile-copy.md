## 11. Trade Company vs Factory Positioning

Recommended positioning:
Factory-direct sourcing partner with a stable local factory network in Shanwei, Guangdong.

Do not say you are a factory or manufacturer unless 汕尾市晶燊缘珠宝有限公司 directly owns and operates the production facility.

Better wording for LinkedIn:

- We work closely with multiple local factories in Shanwei, Guangdong.
- We help overseas buyers source factory-direct natural crystal jewelry and decorative crystal products.
- We provide local supply chain support for wholesale crystal jewelry orders.
- Our advantage is stable factory cooperation, product selection, and flexible wholesale sourcing.

Avoid wording:

- We are the factory.
- We are the original manufacturer.
- Factory owner.
- World's largest gemstone base.

Best short sentence:
We are a Shanwei-based natural crystal jewelry supplier, working closely with local factories to support overseas wholesale buyers.

## 12. Image Usage Suggestions

Recommended image direction:

- Main banner: clean horizontal product scene focused on natural crystal bracelets, bangles, rings, necklaces, and pendants.
- Featured cover: close-up of crystal jewelry trays, bead colors, pendant styles, rings, and organized wholesale sample boxes.
- Auxiliary product image: natural crystal jewelry in the foreground, with crystal chip money trees and crystal geodes as secondary products in the background.
- Product posts: one material or color family per post, such as clear quartz, amethyst, rose quartz, citrine, tiger eye, obsidian, and mixed crystal jewelry.

Avoid image direction:

- Overly mystical crystal healing style.
- Dark fantasy lighting.
- Luxury jewelry branding that looks like retail rather than wholesale.
- Images with fake certificates, fake factory labels, or unreadable AI text.

## 13. Profile Review Checklist

- A buyer can understand within 10 seconds that the main products are natural crystal jewelry items.
- The core categories are clear: bracelets, bangles, rings, necklaces, and pendants.
- The profile clearly targets wholesalers, distributors, importers, and sourcing agents.
- The wording avoids unverifiable claims such as "world's largest", "best", or "guaranteed".
- The profile includes key search terms: natural crystal jewelry wholesale, crystal bracelets, crystal bangles, crystal rings, crystal necklaces, crystal pendants, gemstone wholesale, Guangdong supplier.
- The call to action is simple: message for current styles, wholesale catalog, and sourcing support.
