Stones that are easy to start with

These are common first-collection stones because the colors are easy to see in a photo and easy to mix. 8 mm round beads are a common size. You can mix stones and styles inside the 100-piece order. Color varies because the stones are natural. We confirm the look with you before production.

Citrine

Golden to yellow. A warm color that sits well next to darker beads. A frequent first pick.

Rose quartz

Soft pink. Easy to photograph and easy to pair with purple or clear beads.

Amethyst

Purple, from pale to deep. A familiar shop color and a simple mix with rose quartz.

Tiger eye

Gold-brown with a visible band of light. A strong contrast next to pink or purple.

Lapis lazuli

Deep blue, often with lighter flecks. It reads clearly on a white card. We can quote it with the other stones. Ask if you want a current photo.

Green aventurine

Green with a soft sparkle. Useful when the collection needs a green that is lighter than obsidian. Ask if you want a current photo.

Rainbow obsidian

A dark stone with bands or flashes of color. A different look from the golden obsidian and silver obsidian already on this site. Ask if you want a current photo.

Seven-color mix

Several of these colors on one bracelet. Shops use it as a mixed look. We describe it by the colors in the mix, not by a meaning. 8 mm rounds are a common size for it.

What the customer opens

Three branded pieces. We coordinate them with the beads so you are not chasing separate vendors. Minimums for tags, pouches, boxes and cards are confirmed with the design.

Custom logo tags

A small tag on the bracelet or a hang tag with your logo. Concept image. We confirm what is possible with your artwork.

Branded pouches and gift boxes

A pouch or a box that can carry your brand, ready for an unboxing photo. Concept image. The style is confirmed with your design.

Custom printed cards

A card for your brand story, or a card that names the stone. You send the words. We do not add claims about water, tests, or certificates.
