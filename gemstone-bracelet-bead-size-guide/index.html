Bead Size Guide: 4mm–12mm Gemstone Bracelets and Who They Sell To

What 4mm, 6mm, 8mm, 10mm and 12mm beads actually look and sell like — presence, audience, price drivers and how to choose sizes for your bracelet range, with the sizes JSY Crystal stocks.

Bead diameter is the single strongest design decision in a beaded bracelet: it changes how the piece looks on the wrist, who buys it, what it costs to make, and how it photographs. This guide covers the five sizes you will meet in the market — 4mm through 12mm — and what each one is for. At JSY Crystal, every standard bead size is available across our core stones in the collection — and custom sizes beyond the standard range can be produced to order.

Bead count follows wrist size, not diameter alone

For a standard adult wrist, an 8mm bracelet typically carries around 22 beads, a 6mm around 28, and a 12mm around 18 — the count simply fills the same wrist with bigger or smaller beads. When you order, specify both the bead diameter and the finished wrist size (or bead count), especially for gift programs where recipients vary. Mixed sizes within one group order are normal; our travel and corporate gift orders mix 6mm, 8mm and 10mm in a single shipment.

What size does to price

Buyers often expect price to scale with diameter. It scales faster. A 12mm bead holds roughly three times the material of an 8mm bead, and clean, inclusion-free rough in large sizes is harder to source — which is why large, clean beads of inclusion-rich stones like phantom quartz and rutilated quartz command a premium at 10mm and above. Faceting, when offered, adds labor at every size.

Choosing sizes for a first range

  • Retail and boutique: open with 8mm as the anchor, add 6mm for a lower entry price and gift sets.
  • Men's or unisex lines: 10mm and 12mm carry the range; 8mm works as the lighter option.
  • Gift programs: 8mm suits the widest range of wrists; offer 6mm and 10mm as alternates rather than guessing one size for everyone.
  • Stacking sets: pair 4–6mm pieces in two or three stones; small sizes earn their place in sets, not singles.

Every product page in the shop lists the sizes offered for that stone — start with amethyst, rose quartz and citrine if you want the three most-gifted stones in the classic 8mm.
