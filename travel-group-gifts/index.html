More than a souvenir

A keepsake that carries the trip with it

A bracelet is one of the few trip gifts that leaves the hotel. Chosen well, it becomes the thing your group is still wearing at the reunion — a natural stone picked for the destination, sized for each wrist, and presented in a box with your group's name on the card.

Hosts use JSY group bracelets for the moments that anchor a trip: a welcome gift waiting in the room on arrival, a presentation at the celebration dinner, or a closing keepsake for milestone reunions, incentive trips and retreats. Because every bracelet is made to order, the stone, bead size and card wording are agreed with you first and confirmed on a physical sample — what you approve is what your group receives.

Planning questions

Can a small group ask for a quote?

Yes. Tell us the quantity you need. We will confirm what is feasible for your materials, tag and packaging, including any minimums.

Can you add our destination and year?

Discuss custom tag engraving and printed cards with us. Send your wording or artwork for a feasibility review.

Can I see a sample first?

Sample specifications, charges, shipping and timing are agreed for each project before development.

How far ahead should we contact you?

Send the date you need the gifts and the delivery country. We will confirm a realistic schedule; rush delivery is not assumed.

Can the bracelets be ready as arrival or welcome gifts?

Yes — many groups present the bracelets at the welcome dinner or on arrival day. Share your arrival date and we will plan production and shipping backwards from it, with the sample approved first so there are no surprises.

Can one group order include different stones and sizes?

Yes. Mixed stone types, bead sizes and wrist sizes within a single group order are normal — hosts often choose one stone for the group and let key guests pick a variation.

What makes the gift feel considered rather than generic?

Consistent stone grading across every bracelet, a gift box and a printed card with your group's name, destination or a short message. The details are confirmed on your sample before production.

Do I need separate suppliers for boxes, tags and beads?

No. We coordinate beads, charms, tags, boxes, cards and packaging across our partner workshops in Shanwei, run QC, and ship everything together. You deal with one team from quote to delivery.
