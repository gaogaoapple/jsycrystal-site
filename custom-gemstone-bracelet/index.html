Three ways to work with us

OEM, private label or a new design

OEM to your design

You send the design — a photo, sketch or a bracelet you already sell. We match stones and sizes, make a sample for approval, then produce under your brand. A good fit for brands moving production or adding a second supplier.

Private label from our range

Pick stones and styles from our catalog, add your logo tag, cards and packaging, and launch faster. Details on the private label page.

Develop something new

Have a market or a theme but no design yet? Tell us who the bracelets are for and your target price. We suggest stone combinations and bead sizes and sample two or three options.

Small first orders are welcome: the minimum is 10 pieces per order, freely mixed. Printed or branded parts may have their own minimums; we confirm them in the quote.

Need other bead materials or crystal items? Tell us what you have in mind and we'll check what we can make or source. Natural stone bracelets stay our main line, and we'll tell you honestly if something isn't a good fit.

Process

How a custom order runs

  1. Send your brief

    Stones, bead size, quantity, delivery country and any reference photos. Rough ideas are fine.

  2. Quote and suggestions

    We reply within 24 hours with stone and design suggestions, pricing guidance and timing.

  3. Sample

    We make the sample and send photos and video. Sample fees and shipping are quoted for your project.

  4. Approval

    You confirm stones, size, finish, tags and packaging. The approved sample becomes the reference.

  5. Bulk production

    Usually 15–30 days after approval, depending on quantity and customization.

  6. QC and shipping

    Pieces are checked before packing. DHL or FedEx in about 7–10 days, or sea freight in about 25–35 days.

FAQ

Custom bracelet questions

What is the minimum order for custom gemstone bracelets?

Our minimum is 10 pieces per order, and you can mix stones, sizes and styles within that total. Branded parts such as printed cards, boxes or logo tags can have their own minimums, which we confirm when we quote your project.

Can I get a sample before the bulk order?

Yes. We make a physical sample to your specification and send photos and video for review. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project, and any credit against your bulk order is written into the quotation.

How long does production take?

Bulk production usually takes 15–30 days after sample approval, depending on quantity and how much is customized. Express shipping by DHL or FedEx takes about 7–10 days; sea freight takes about 25–35 days.

Can you put my brand on the bracelets and packaging?

Yes. We can add your logo on hang tags, small pendants or tags on the bracelet, printed product cards, pouches and gift boxes. See the private label program for the branding options.

Do you work as an OEM manufacturer for other brands?

Yes. Send a reference photo, a sketch or an existing product and we produce it to your specification under your brand. Designs and customer details are kept confidential, and an NDA is available on request.

Do you make stone-set bracelets or only beaded ones?

Our core product is natural stone beaded bracelets on elastic or knotted cord, with optional spacers, charms and tags. If you need a different construction, send a photo of the reference and we will tell you honestly whether we can make it.

Which stones can I choose?

We work with 20+ natural stones, including amethyst, rose quartz, strawberry quartz, citrine, clear quartz, tiger eye, black obsidian, golden and silver obsidian, gold rutilated quartz, phantom quartz and super seven. If a stone is not listed, ask — we can usually source it.

Do all stones in a batch look the same?

They are natural, so colour and inclusions vary between beads and between batches. We grade beads by colour and size before stringing, and for repeat orders we keep your approved sample as the reference.

Can you make bracelets in other bead materials, or other crystal items?

Natural stone beaded bracelets are our main line. If you need other bead materials or crystal items, tell us what you have in mind and we'll check what we can make or source, and say so plainly if it isn't a good fit.

Do I need separate suppliers for boxes, tags and beads?

No. We coordinate beads, charms, tags, boxes, cards and packaging across our partner workshops in Shanwei, run QC, and ship everything together. You deal with one team from quote to delivery.
