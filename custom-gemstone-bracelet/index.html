What a custom gemstone bracelet order looks like

Most custom gemstone bracelet and custom crystal bracelet orders start one of three ways.

Your design, made to spec

Send a photo, a sketch, or a bracelet you already sell. We match the stone and bead size, then produce it under your brand. This suits shops adding a supplier or moving an existing line. See OEM bracelet manufacturing.

Our styles, your label

Pick bracelets from the stones on this page, add your logo tag, card and box, and you have a branded line. See private label options.

A new idea, worked out together

You know your customer but not the exact design. Tell us who the bracelets are for. We suggest stone combinations and bead sizes, and you approve the spec before the order.

One stone or a mix. Start from the stones below, or name another natural stone and we will say what is available.

Custom gemstone bracelet FAQ

Do you make custom gemstone bracelets for individual orders?

No. We work with shops, boutiques, gift stores and brands. Finished bracelet orders start at 10 pieces, and you can mix stones and styles within that. We don't take single retail orders.

What is the minimum order for custom bracelets?

10 finished bracelets, mixed across stones, sizes and styles. Logo tags, cards and boxes have their own minimums, which we confirm when we quote your design.

Can I mix custom crystal bracelets with designs from your range?

Yes. Many buyers combine one or two custom designs with stones already shown on this site in the same order.

Can you put my logo on the bracelet and packaging?

Yes: a small logo tag or charm on the bracelet, a hang tag or card, and pouches or gift boxes. What is possible, and the minimums, are confirmed with your design.

Can I get a sample before the full order?

If a sample is needed, fees and shipping are quoted for your project.

How long does a custom order take?

Production and shipping times are confirmed with your order, because they depend on the stone, the quantity and the tags or packaging you choose.

Do you make stone-set bracelets?

No. Our line is natural stone beaded bracelets on elastic or knotted cord, with optional metal spacers, charms and tags. Stone-set pieces in metal settings are a different kind of work. If you are not sure, send a photo and we will tell you plainly.

Will every bracelet look exactly like the sample?

The stones are natural, so colour and inclusions vary between beads and between batches. Ask for photos of the current batch before you confirm.

Are the bracelets waterproof?

No. Natural stone bracelets on cord should not be soaked, worn for swimming, or left in sweat. If you want a care note on your card, say so in the inquiry.

Do I need separate suppliers for beads, tags and boxes?

No. Our team coordinates beads, tags and packaging with partner workshops in Ketang, Shanwei. You work with one contact.
