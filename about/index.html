About JSY Crystal

One partner for your bracelet program,
beads to packaging

JSY Crystal Jewelry is the export brand of Shanwei Jingshenyuan Jewelry Co., Ltd., based in Ketang, Shanwei, Guangdong. We are a one-stop custom bracelet solution supplier and the exclusive overseas agent of our partner factories there — you deal with one team for beads, charms, tags, boxes, cards, QC and shipping.

What we do

We supply natural crystal bracelets and semi-precious stone jewelry to B2B buyers: jewelry brands, boutiques, wholesalers, designers, wellness and gift brands, and corporate and travel gift programs. We work with partner factories and workshops in Ketang, Shanwei: they concentrate on production; JSY takes the overseas order, sources across those partners, and runs the export side in English.

The headline benefit is one-stop. You do not need separate suppliers for beads, charms, tags, gift boxes and cards — we coordinate them, approve samples, check quality and ship everything together. Background on how the workshops work is on the partner factories page.

What we handle for you

You deal with one partner. We coordinate beads, accessories, branding and packaging across our Shanwei partner workshops, check quality, and ship everything together.

  • Beads and stones — natural stone first; other bead materials on request
  • Charms and accessories
  • Tags and engraving — logo or name tags
  • Gift boxes, cards and packaging
  • Sample approval
  • Quality checks (QC)
  • Consolidated shipping

What we supply

  • Eight core bracelet stones: amethyst, citrine, clear quartz, four seasons phantom quartz, gold rutilated quartz, golden obsidian, silver obsidian and rose quartz — plus the full range of natural crystal and semi-precious stone types on request. If it exists as a crystal bracelet, we can produce it.
  • Bead sizes: the full standard range, with custom sizes and packaging available.
  • Programs: private label jewelry, custom gemstone bracelets, corporate gifts and travel group keepsakes.

How orders run

  • Exclusive overseas agent of our partner factories in Ketang, Shanwei — one English-speaking team for your account.
  • Minimum order 10 pieces per order, freely mixed across styles, stones and sizes.
  • Samples charged at cost (buyer covers shipping), with the sample fee deductible from a first order that reaches the qualifying quantity.
  • Bulk production in 15–30 days; pre-shipment inspection arrangements supported.
  • Worldwide shipping by DHL, FedEx or sea freight; inquiries answered within 24 hours.
  • Custom cards and packaging, private-label pendants and tags, and a stable replenishment cycle for repeat lines.

Who you deal with

One dedicated contact on our overseas team handles your order. The person who quotes it is the person who follows it through sourcing, quality checks and dispatch — no hand-offs between departments. Reach us any time by email at marketing@jsycrystal.com.
