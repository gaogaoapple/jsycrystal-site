About JSY Crystal

Based in Ketang, working with partner factories

JSY Crystal Jewelry is the export brand of Shanwei Jingshenyuan Jewelry Co., Ltd., based in Ketang, Shanwei, Guangdong. We coordinate natural-stone bracelet orders with partner factories in this industry cluster. JSY is not a self-owned factory. You work with one team on finished bracelets and, when needed, logo tags, tassels and packaging.

What we do

We supply finished natural-stone bracelets to B2B buyers — especially crystal shops, boutiques, gift stores, wellness retailers and wholesale buyers. Partner factories in Ketang concentrate on production. JSY takes the overseas order, coordinates those partners, and handles the buyer-facing side in English. Being based in Ketang means we work inside a cluster that includes raw material, processing, trading and beading workshops. That local setting is not a claim that JSY owns those factories or every process in the town.

Background on the local workshops is on the partner factories page.

What we handle for you

You deal with one partner. We coordinate beads, accessories, branding and packaging across our Shanwei partner workshops, and ship everything together.

  • Beads and stones — natural stone first; other bead materials on request
  • Charms and accessories
  • Tags and engraving — logo or name tags
  • Gift boxes, cards and packaging
  • Sample approval
  • Consolidated shipping

What we supply

  • Eight core bracelet stones: amethyst, citrine, clear quartz, four seasons phantom quartz, gold rutilated quartz, golden obsidian, silver obsidian and rose quartz — plus the full range of natural crystal and semi-precious stone types on request. If it exists as a crystal bracelet, we can produce it.
  • Bead sizes: the full standard range, with custom sizes and packaging available.
  • Programs: private label jewelry, custom gemstone bracelets, corporate gifts and travel group keepsakes.

How we work together

This is a short buying path, not a case study.

  1. Tell us your shop type, the style you want, and your estimated quantity.
  2. Share the stone, size, tags or packaging you have in mind, if you already know them. Bead sizes shown on this site, such as 6–12 mm, are examples only.
  3. We send a quote. Timing is confirmed with your order. Minimums and feasibility for logo tags, packaging and other custom details are confirmed with the design.
  4. If a sample is needed, sample options, fees and shipping are quoted for your project.
  5. After approval, we coordinate the order with partner factories and arrange shipment.

Tell us your shop type, the style you want, and your estimated quantity. We will follow up as soon as possible from marketing@jsycrystal.com.
