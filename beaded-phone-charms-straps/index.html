Before you order

Sweat, water and daily use

A phone charm lives in the hand. That means oil, sweat and the odd splash, which is a harder life than a bracelet kept in a box. Natural stone should be kept away from sweat, pool water and long water exposure. We do not describe natural stone as waterproof or sweat-proof. Shops can tell customers to keep the beads dry when they can, and to take the charm off before a workout or a swim.

Hooks, clasps, rings and other metal parts are stainless steel as standard, so they are waterproof and tarnish-free. If a design uses gold-plated brass for a warmer colour, those parts are rust-proof and waterproof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free.

Process

From idea to a packed shipment

  1. Send your idea

    Stones and colours, whether you want a charm or a strap, a photo of a piece or a case you like, a rough quantity and your logo file if you have one is enough to start.

  2. Mockup and quote

    We reply as soon as possible with questions, a quotation and a mockup showing beads, length, attachment, tassel, tag and packaging.

  3. Sample approval

    If a sample is needed, fees and shipping are quoted for your project. Production starts after you approve the specification.

  4. Production

    After you approve the specification, the pieces are strung and finished. Bulk work usually takes 15–30 days, depending on quantity and how much is customized.

  5. Packing

    Packing is arranged with the order.

  6. Consolidated shipping

    Charms, tags and boxes leave together. DHL or FedEx takes about 7–10 days; sea freight about 25–35 days for larger orders.

FAQ

Phone charm and strap questions

What is the minimum order for beaded phone charms?

10 pieces per order in total. You can mix phone charms, phone straps, stones and colours inside those 10 pieces, and add bag charms, keychains or bracelets to the same order. Branded extras such as engraved logo tags, printed cards or custom boxes can have their own minimums, and we confirm those in your quotation.

How much do custom phone charms cost?

Pricing depends on quantity, design and packaging, so we quote each inquiry. Tell us the stones, the length and style, a rough quantity and whether you need logo tags or boxes, and the quotation will show every line clearly.

What is the difference between a beaded phone charm and a phone strap?

A phone charm is a short beaded strand that clips to a phone, often finished with a small charm, a tassel or a logo tag. A phone strap is a beaded wrist or short strap that also clips to a phone, so it can sit in the hand or on the wrist. Length and attachment are agreed on the mockup and sample. If you have a particular case or clip in mind, send a photo with your inquiry.

Can I mix phone charms with bag charms and keychains?

Yes. Mixing is normal for a first order. Many shops start with a small spread of phone charms, bag charms, keychains and stones, see what sells, then reorder the ones that work. See also our bag charms and keychains page.

Can you add our logo or shop name?

Yes. Your logo or name can go on a small tag or charm on the strand, a hang tag, a printed card or a gift box. Artwork is reviewed with your mockup and sample before production.

Are natural-stone phone charms waterproof or sweat-proof?

No. Natural stone should be kept away from sweat, pool water and long water exposure. We do not describe natural stone as waterproof or sweat-proof. A phone is held in the hand all day, so tell your customers to keep the beads dry when they can. Stainless steel clasps, rings and findings are waterproof and tarnish-free. If a design uses gold-plated brass, those parts are rust-proof and waterproof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free.

Can I order a sample first?

If a sample is needed, fees and shipping are quoted for your project.

How long does a phone charm order take?

Bulk production usually runs 15–30 days after you approve the sample, depending on quantity and how much is customized. Shipping is added on top: about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or about 25–35 days by sea freight.

Do I need separate suppliers for charms, tags and boxes?

No. We coordinate beads, hardware, tassels, tags, boxes, cards and packaging across our partner workshops in Ketang, Shanwei, and ship everything together. You deal with one team from quote to delivery.
