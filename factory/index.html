The partnership

Why "exclusive agent" works better for buyers

Our partner factories and workshops in Ketang, Shanwei concentrate on producing natural crystal and semi-precious stone bracelets. They have no overseas sales team of their own — by design, that side is JSY Crystal. As their exclusive overseas agent, we take every export order from first inquiry to final delivery, source across those partners, and pass production requirements directly to the workshops.

For buyers, the headline benefit is one-stop: you do not need separate suppliers for beads, charms, tags, gift boxes and cards. We coordinate them, approve samples, check quality and ship everything together, with an English-speaking team that answers within 24 hours and stays responsible after the order ships.

What we handle for you

You deal with one partner. Across our Shanwei partner workshops we coordinate:

  • Beads and stones — natural stone first; other bead materials on request
  • Charms and accessories
  • Tags and engraving — logo or name tags
  • Gift boxes, cards and packaging
  • Sample approval
  • Quality checks (QC)
  • Consolidated shipping

Inside production

From rough stone to finished bracelet

  1. 01 · Material selection

    Rough natural stone is selected and sorted for color and clarity before cutting — the step that decides the grade of everything after it.

  2. 02 · Cutting & shaping

    Stone is cut and shaped into beads to the ordered diameter, across the full range of bead sizes, including custom.

  3. 03 · Polishing & drilling

    Beads are polished for surface finish and drilled cleanly, so stringing sits smooth and wears comfortably.

  4. 04 · Grading & size sorting

    Finished beads are graded and sorted by color, clarity and exact size, so every bracelet in a batch matches.

  5. 05 · Stringing & finishing

    Bracelets are strung to the specified wrist size and bead count, with custom stone combinations and spacer details as ordered.

  6. 06 · QC & packing

    Counts, sizing, tension and finish are checked before packing; pre-shipment inspection arrangements are supported.

Factory questions

Is JSY Crystal a factory or a trading company?

JSY Crystal is the exclusive overseas agent of our partner factories in Ketang, Shanwei. The workshops produce the bracelets; they have no export team of their own, so we handle communication, samples, quality checks and shipping for overseas buyers. We also coordinate packaging and accessories as a one-stop program, so you deal with one team from quote to delivery.

Can I inspect the goods before shipping?

Yes. We support pre-shipment inspection arrangements, and inspection photos or video can be provided before dispatch so you can verify grading, sizing and counts.

Can the factory produce custom designs, sizes and packaging?

Yes. Custom bead sizes, stone combinations, private-label pendants and tags, printed cards and gift packaging are part of the normal program. Specifications are confirmed with a physical sample before bulk production.

What are the production lead times?

Bulk production runs 15–30 days depending on quantity and customization, plus shipping — about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or 25–35 days by sea freight.
