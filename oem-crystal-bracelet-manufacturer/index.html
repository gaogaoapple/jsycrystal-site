What OEM means here

You own the design. We make it.

OEM is the right route when you already know what the bracelet should look like. Maybe you are moving a best-seller from another supplier, adding a second source, or turning your own sketch into stock. You send the reference; we work out the stones, bead count and finish, and sample it.

If you would rather pick from bracelets we already make and add your logo, that is private label. If you have an idea but no design yet, start on the custom bracelet page. All three routes use the same production and the same 10-piece minimum.

Process

How an OEM order runs

  1. Send the reference

    A photo, a sample piece or a spec, plus estimated quantity and delivery country.

  2. Quote

    We reply within 24 hours with questions, pricing guidance and timing.

  3. Sample

    We make the sample and send photos and video. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project.

  4. Approve

    You sign off stones, size, count, tags and packaging. That sample becomes the reference for this order and for reorders.

  5. Produce

    Bulk production usually takes 15–30 days, depending on quantity and customization.

  6. Check and ship

    Counts, sizing, cord tension and finish are checked before packing. Inspection photos or video can be sent before dispatch. DHL or FedEx in about 7–10 days, sea freight in about 25–35 days.

Repeat orders

How we keep the tenth order like the first

For an OEM buyer, the second order matters more than the first. Your customers expect the bracelet they bought last season.

  • Consistent production partners. Reorders use the same partner workshops and the same grading rules.
  • Grading before stringing. Beads are sorted by colour, clarity and exact size, so bracelets in one batch match each other.
  • The approved sample is the standard. We keep its spec — stone, size, count, cord, tag — and check reorders against it.
  • Plain talk about stones. Natural stones vary. If a stone is treated or dyed, we tell you on the quote, and we will send photos of the current batch before you order.

FAQ

OEM bracelet questions

What is the minimum order for OEM crystal bracelets?

Our minimum is 10 pieces per order, and you can mix stones, sizes and designs within that total. Branded parts such as engraved tags, printed cards or custom boxes can have their own minimums; we confirm those in your quotation before you commit.

Can you copy a bracelet I already sell?

Yes, that is the most common OEM brief. Send a clear photo or post us a piece. We match the stone, bead size, bead count and spacers, make a sample, and you compare it side by side with your original before bulk production.

How long does an OEM order take?

Bulk production usually runs 15–30 days after you approve the sample, depending on quantity and how much is customized. Shipping is added on top: about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or about 25–35 days by sea freight.

Do I get a sample before paying for bulk?

Yes. Every OEM order starts with a physical sample, and we send photos and video of it. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project, and any credit against your bulk order is written into the quotation.

Are you a factory or a trading company?

JSY Crystal is the exclusive overseas agent of our partner factories in Ketang, Shanwei. The workshops produce the bracelets; they have no export team of their own, so we handle communication, samples, quality checks and shipping for overseas buyers. We also coordinate packaging and accessories, so you deal with one team from quote to delivery.

Will my design stay confidential?

Yes. We do not show or sell your design to other buyers, and an NDA is available on request.

Will every bracelet look exactly like the sample?

The size, bead count, cord and finish will match. The stones are natural, so colour and inclusions vary a little from bead to bead and batch to batch. We grade beads by colour and size before stringing and keep your approved sample as the reference for reorders.

Can you make bracelets in other bead materials, or other crystal items?

Natural stone bracelets are our main line. If you need other bead materials or crystal items, tell us what you have in mind and we'll check what we can make or source, and say so plainly if it is not a good fit.

Do I need separate suppliers for boxes, tags and beads?

No. We coordinate beads, charms, tags, boxes, cards and packaging across our partner workshops in Shanwei, run QC, and ship everything together. You deal with one team from quote to delivery.
