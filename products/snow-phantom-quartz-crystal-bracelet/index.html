Snow Phantom Quartz Bracelets — Wholesale & Custom

Snow phantom quartz is clear quartz with white, cloud-like layers inside — the 'phantoms' look like drifting snow. The result is a clean, minimal bracelet that suits neutral and white-tone collections.

Buying notes

  • Each bead shows a different phantom pattern; no two bracelets are identical.
  • Pairs well with clear quartz, four seasons phantom and gold rutilated quartz.
  • Higher grades show clearer quartz and more defined phantoms; ask for batch photos.
