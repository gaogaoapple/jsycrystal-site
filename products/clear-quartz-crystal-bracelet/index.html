FAQ

Clear Quartz bracelet questions

What is the minimum order for these bracelets?

100 pieces mixed per order. Logo tags and boxes are confirmed with the design.

Can I mix this stone with others?

Yes. Mix stones and styles inside those 100 pieces. See the other stone pages and tiger eye below.

Can you make a custom size or add a logo tag?

Yes. Bead size and a logo tag are confirmed with the design. A customer design can be kept exclusive to that customer. For a full private label order, see private label.

Will every bracelet be the same colour?

Natural clear quartz can show small inclusions, and clarity varies from bead to bead and from batch to batch. Colour and pattern vary by batch; we confirm details with you before production.

Are these bracelets waterproof?

No. They are not waterproof. Take them off for swimming and showers.
