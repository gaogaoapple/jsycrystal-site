Strawberry Quartz Bracelets — Wholesale & Custom

Strawberry quartz is a pink quartz with tiny red to reddish-brown inclusions that give each bead a speckled, strawberry-like look. It sells well next to rose quartz in pink and gift assortments, and it mixes easily with clear quartz and amethyst.
