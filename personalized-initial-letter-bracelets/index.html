What we make

Natural stone, with a letter that is yours to specify

These bracelets use the same natural stones and the same production team as our wholesale crystal bracelets. The difference is the letter: a single initial, letter beads that spell a name or a short word, or an engraved letter on a small tag. Send a reference photo or a short brief, including the letters or names you want, and we will say what we can make.

  • Initial charm bracelets — a natural-stone strand with one letter charm. Shops often mix different letters in the same order so the counter can show more than one initial.
  • Letter bead bracelets — natural stone with letter beads in the strand, for a name or a short word. Send the spelling with your inquiry; we show it on the mockup and the sample before production.
  • Engraved letter on a tag or charm — a small tag with an initial, or with your shop or brand name, on a natural-stone bracelet.
  • Logo tags and packaging — your shop or brand name on a hang tag, a printed card or a gift box, in the same shipment.

Letter charms and engraved tags can be made in stainless steel or gold-plated brass, as agreed on the quotation and the sample. Other letter bead materials can be checked on request. We mark what was used so you know what you are selling.

Wrist length is usually about 15–20 cm; longer lengths and 10 mm or 12 mm beads are available when you need them. You can also add mixed metal beaded bracelets, stackable sets or custom all-stone bracelets to the same order.

Before you order

Sweat, water and the letter parts

An initial or letter bracelet is worn on the wrist, so it meets sweat, lotion and the odd splash. Natural stone should be kept away from sweat, pool water and long water exposure. We do not describe natural stone as waterproof or sweat-proof. Shops can tell customers to take the bracelet off before a workout, a swim or a shower.

Stainless steel letter charms and tags are waterproof and tarnish-free. Gold-plated brass parts are rust-proof and waterproof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free. If a design uses other letter bead materials, we note the material and any care points on the quotation and the sample.

Process

From idea to a packed shipment

  1. Send your idea

    Stones and colours, whether you want initial charms, letter beads or an engraved tag, the letters or names, a reference photo if you have one, a rough quantity and your logo file if you have one is enough to start.

  2. Mockup and quote

    We reply as soon as possible with questions, a quotation and a mockup showing beads, letter parts, spelling, length, tag and packaging.

  3. Sample approval

    If a sample is needed, fees and shipping are quoted for your project. Check the stones, the letter parts and the spelling before you approve the specification. Production starts after that approval.

  4. Production

    Beads are strung and finished with the agreed letter parts to the specification you approved. Bulk work usually takes 15–30 days, depending on quantity and how much is customized.

  5. Packing

    Packing is arranged with the order.

  6. Consolidated shipping

    Bracelets, tags and boxes leave together. Shipping method and carrier are discussed per order. Our quotes are EXW Ketang, Shanwei.

What is the minimum order for personalized initial or letter bracelets?

How much do personalized initial or letter bead bracelets cost?

Pricing depends on quantity, design and packaging, so we quote each inquiry. Tell us whether you want initial charms, letter beads or an engraved tag, the letters or names, the stones, a rough quantity and whether you need logo tags or boxes, and the quotation will show every line clearly.

What is the difference between an initial charm bracelet and a letter bead bracelet?

An initial charm bracelet is a natural-stone strand with one letter charm, or with an engraved letter on a small tag. A letter bead bracelet uses letter beads in the strand to spell a name or a short word. Send a reference photo if you have a look in mind, and we will say what we can make.

Can I mix different letters or names in one order?

Yes. Mixing is normal for a first order. Tell us which letters or names you want and how many of each, if you already know. You do not have to order every letter. Many shops start with a small spread of initials and stones, see what sells, then reorder the ones that work.

Can you spell a name or a short word with letter beads?

Yes. Send the names or words with your inquiry. We show the spelling on the mockup and the sample, and production follows the specification you approved. A longer name may need a different bead size or wrist length; we will say so before you approve.

Are initial or letter bead bracelets waterproof or sweat-proof?

No. Natural stone should be kept away from sweat, pool water and long water exposure. We do not describe natural stone as waterproof or sweat-proof. Tell your customers to take the bracelet off before a workout, a swim or a shower. Stainless steel letter charms and tags are waterproof and tarnish-free. If a design uses gold-plated brass, those parts are rust-proof and waterproof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free. If other letter bead materials are used, we note the material and any care points on the quotation and the sample.

Can you add our logo or shop name?

Yes. Your logo or name can go on a small tag or charm on the bracelet, a hang tag, a printed card or a gift box. Artwork is reviewed with your mockup and sample before production.
