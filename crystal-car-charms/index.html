What we make

Mirror charms, suncatchers and matching sets

Car charms use the same natural stones and the same partner workshops as our wholesale crystal bracelets and beaded bag charms and keychains. What changes is the length, the way it hangs, and where it lives: in a car, in the sun, for months at a time. That last part shapes how we suggest stones and hardware.

  • Rearview mirror charms — a short strand of natural-stone beads on a cord loop that goes over the mirror arm, often finished with a tassel, a single larger bead or a small charm.
  • Crystal suncatchers — a beaded strand ending in a faceted prism that throws light into the car or a window. The beads can be natural stone; the classic prism is usually glass and is made on request.
  • Car and key sets — a mirror charm with a matching keychain or bag charm in the same stones, boxed together as one gift.
  • Charm plus bracelet gift sets — a car charm boxed with a matching custom bracelet, for shops that sell sets at the counter.
  • Logo tags and cards — your shop or brand name on a small tag on the strand, a hang tag, or a printed card.

Before you order

Sun, heat and the driver's view

A car charm has a harder life than a bracelet in a drawer. A car parked outside gets hot, and the charm hangs in direct light through the windshield all day. Some natural stones change colour after long direct sun: amethyst, rose quartz and citrine are the common ones, and dyed beads can fade as well. So we do not promise that a car charm will not fade. If colour is the main selling point, tell us, and we will suggest stones that usually hold their look better in light, such as clear quartz, tiger eye or obsidian, and confirm the choice on the sample.

Hooks, rings and other metal parts are stainless steel as standard, so they do not rust or tarnish. If a design uses gold-plated brass for a warmer colour, those parts are rust-proof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free.

The other point is the driver. A long, heavy strand can swing into the windshield on a rough road or sit in the line of sight. We usually keep mirror charms short and use lighter beads, and we suggest shops tell customers to check local rules on items hanging from the mirror, since those rules differ from place to place.

Process

From idea to a packed shipment

  1. Send your idea

    Stones and colours, the length you want, a photo of a charm you like, a rough quantity and your logo file if you have one is enough to start.

  2. Mockup and quote

    We reply as soon as possible with questions, a quotation and a mockup showing beads, loop, tassel or prism, tag and packaging.

  3. Sample approval

    We make a sample and send photos and video with it. Hang it in your own car before you approve it. Production starts after approval.

  4. Production

    Beads are graded by colour and size, then strung and finished to the approved sample. Bulk work usually takes 15–30 days, depending on quantity and how much is customized.

  5. QC

    We check counts, lengths, knots, attachments and packing, and can send inspection photos before dispatch.

  6. Consolidated shipping

    Charms, tags and boxes leave together. DHL or FedEx takes about 7–10 days; sea freight about 25–35 days for larger orders.

FAQ

Car charm questions

What is the minimum order for crystal car charms?

10 pieces per order in total. You can mix mirror charms, suncatchers, stones and colours inside those 10 pieces, and add bag charms, keychains or bracelets to the same order. Branded extras such as engraved logo tags, printed cards or custom boxes can have their own minimums, and we confirm those in your quotation.

How much do custom car charms cost?

Pricing depends on quantity, design and packaging, so we quote each inquiry. Tell us the stones, the length and style, a rough quantity and whether you need logo tags or boxes, and the quotation will show every line clearly.

Will the stones fade in a hot car?

Some can. A car parked in the sun gets hot and the charm sits in direct light through the windshield. Amethyst, rose quartz and citrine can lose colour after long direct sun, and dyed beads can fade too. We do not promise that a car charm will not fade. If colour matters most, ask us for stones that usually hold their look better in light, and tell your customers to expect some change over time.

Are crystal suncatchers made of natural stone?

The beaded strand can be natural stone. The faceted prism at the end of a classic suncatcher is usually glass, and we make that on request. We describe each material as it is on the quote and on your product cards.

Can you add our logo or shop name?

Yes. Your logo or name can go on a small tag or charm on the strand, a hang tag, a printed card or a gift box. Artwork is reviewed with your mockup and sample before production.

Is it safe to hang a charm from the rearview mirror?

Keep it short and light so it does not block the driver's view or swing into the windshield. Rules on items hanging from the mirror differ from place to place, so we suggest shops tell customers to check their local rules. We can make shorter lengths and lighter beads for this reason.

Can I order a sample first?

Yes. After we agree a mockup, we can send a sample with photos and video. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project, and if any of the sample fee is credited against your bulk order, the quotation says so.

How long does a car charm order take?

Bulk production usually runs 15–30 days after you approve the sample, depending on quantity and how much is customized. Shipping is added on top: about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or about 25–35 days by sea freight.

Do I need separate suppliers for charms, tags and boxes?

No. We coordinate beads, hardware, tassels, tags, boxes, cards and packaging across our partner workshops in Ketang, Shanwei, run QC, and ship everything together. You deal with one team from quote to delivery.
