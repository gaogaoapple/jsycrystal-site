Order steps

How a custom bracelet order works

Five steps from your note to a shipment at your US address. We manufacture your bracelets in Ketang. Production and shipping timelines are on the OEM page.

Get a quote

The flow

Send inquiry, quote, sample, production, ship

1. Send inquiry

Email the stones or style, a rough quantity, and the US address the order should reach. A short note is enough.

2. Quote by email

We reply by email with a quote for that order. Pricing depends on quantity, design and packaging; we quote each inquiry.

3. Sample, if needed

Concept image. A sample is optional. If you need one, the fee and shipping are quoted. The sample fee can be credited against the bulk order.

4. Production

After you approve, We manufacture your bracelets in Ketang. Finished bracelets start at 10 pieces per order, mixed.

5. Ex-works handover

Concept image. Our quotes are EXW Ketang, Shanwei. Shipping method and carrier are discussed per order. The buyer, or their forwarder, handles freight, import duties and customs clearance. Export papers can include a commercial invoice, a packing list and a certificate of origin.

FAQ

Questions about the steps

What is the minimum order for finished bracelets?

Finished bracelets start at 10 pieces per order, mixed across styles and stones. For other products, packaging and tags, tell us the quantity you need and we will confirm.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. If a sample is needed, fees and shipping are quoted for your project. The sample fee can be credited against the bulk order.

Do you ship to the US?

Our quotes are EXW Ketang, Shanwei. Shipping method and carrier are discussed per order. The buyer, or their forwarder, handles freight, import duties and customs clearance. Export papers can include a commercial invoice, a packing list and a certificate of origin.
