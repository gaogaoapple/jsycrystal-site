Why a 10-piece minimum matters

Test a range before you commit to it

Many crystal bracelet suppliers ask for 50 or 100 pieces per design. That works for a chain store. It does not work for a shop that wants to see whether rose quartz outsells citrine on its own counter.

Our minimum is 10 pieces per order, counted across the whole order, not per stone. A first order can be 10 amethyst, or two or three each of four stones in two bead sizes. When you know what sells, reorder those in larger numbers and drop the rest. If you later want a design only your shop carries, the same order route covers custom bracelets and OEM production.

Process

How a wholesale order runs

  1. Tell us what you need

    Stones, bead sizes, a rough quantity, packaging and delivery country. A rough idea is enough.

  2. Quote

    We reply within 24 hours with questions, suggestions and a quotation for your mix.

  3. Sample

    If you want to see the bracelets first, we send a sample with photos and video. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project.

  4. Produce and check

    Beads are graded by colour and size before stringing. Counts, sizing and cord tension are checked before packing, and inspection photos can be sent before dispatch.

  5. Ship

    DHL or FedEx in about 7–10 days, or sea freight in about 25–35 days for larger orders. See importing crystal bracelets from China for what first-time importers should plan for.

FAQ

Wholesale crystal bracelet questions

What is the minimum order for wholesale crystal bracelets?

10 pieces per order in total. You can mix stones, bead sizes and styles inside those 10 pieces, so a shop can try amethyst, rose quartz and citrine side by side without buying 50 of each. Branded extras such as engraved tags, printed cards or custom boxes can have their own minimums, and we confirm those in your quotation.

How much do wholesale crystal bracelets cost?

Pricing depends on quantity, design and packaging, so we quote each inquiry. Tell us the stones, bead size, rough quantity and whether you need tags or boxes, and the quotation will show every line clearly.

Can I mix different stones in one wholesale order?

Yes. Mixing is normal for a first order. Most shops start with a spread of stones and sizes, see what sells, then reorder the best sellers in larger numbers.

Can I order a sample before buying wholesale?

Yes. We can send a sample first, with photos and video of it. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project, and if any of the sample fee is credited against your bulk order, the quotation says so.

How long does a wholesale order take?

Bulk production usually runs 15–30 days after you approve the sample, depending on quantity and how much is customized. Shipping is added on top: about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or about 25–35 days by sea freight.

Are the stones natural?

Our main line is natural stone. Natural stones vary in colour and inclusions from bead to bead and batch to batch. If a stone is treated or dyed, we say so on the quote, and we can send photos of the current batch before you order.

Can you put my shop's name or logo on the bracelets?

Yes. Your logo can go on a hang tag, a small tag or pendant on the bracelet, a printed card or a gift box. If you want a full private label range, see our private label page.

Do I need separate suppliers for boxes, tags and beads?

No. We coordinate beads, charms, tags, boxes, cards and packaging across our partner workshops in Shanwei, run QC, and ship everything together. You deal with one team from quote to delivery.

Can you supply other bead materials or crystal items?

Natural stone bracelets are our main line. If you need other bead materials or crystal items, tell us what you have in mind and we'll check what we can make or source, and say so plainly if it is not a good fit.
