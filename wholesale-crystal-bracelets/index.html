How a wholesale order works

  1. Send request

    Stones or style, a rough quantity, and the ship-to country. A short note is enough.

    We quote the mix. If a sample is needed, fees and shipping are quoted. The sample fee can be credited against the bulk order.

    After you approve, JSY makes the order in Ketang.

    Beads, tags and packaging ship together. Timing is confirmed with your order. See importing crystal bracelets from China.

FAQ

FAQ for retailers

What is the minimum order for wholesale crystal bracelets?

Finished bracelets start at 10 pieces per order, mixed across styles and stones. The 10-piece minimum is for finished bracelets, not for logo tags or boxes. For other products, tell us the quantity and we will confirm.

Can I mix stones and styles in one order?

Yes. Finished bracelets can mix stones and styles in one order, inside the 10-piece total.

Do you ship to the US?

Our quotes are EXW Ketang, Shanwei. Shipping method and carrier are discussed per order. The buyer, or their forwarder, handles freight, import duties and customs clearance.

Who pays import duties?

Our quotes are EXW. The buyer, or their forwarder, handles freight, import duties and customs clearance.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. If a sample is needed, fees and shipping are quoted for your project. The sample fee can be credited against the bulk order.

Can you keep my design exclusive / sign an NDA?

Yes. A customer design can be kept exclusive to that customer. We can sign an NDA.

What can a private label order include?

Your logo can go on a hang tag, a small tag or pendant, a printed card or a gift box. We provide resellers with a product image pack. Minimums for branded extras are confirmed in the quote. See private label.
