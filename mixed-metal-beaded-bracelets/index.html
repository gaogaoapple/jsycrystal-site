What we make

Natural stone, mixed with metal

These bracelets use the same natural stones and the same partner workshops as our wholesale crystal bracelets. The difference is the metal: spacers, tubes, accent pieces, clasps and findings in stainless steel or gold-plated brass, combined with the beads to the design you send. We keep this page general on purpose — send a reference photo or a short brief, and we will say what we can make.

  • Natural stone with stainless steel — a clean, silver-tone look. Those metal parts are waterproof and tarnish-free.
  • Natural stone with gold-plated brass — a warmer, gold-look bracelet. If you are looking for gold beaded bracelet wholesale, this is usually natural stone with gold-plated brass parts, not a solid-gold piece. Those metal parts are rust-proof and waterproof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free.
  • Mixed finishes in one order — some pieces in stainless steel and some in gold-plated brass, if that is what you want, alongside all-stone bracelets or stackable sets.
  • Logo tags and packaging — your shop or brand name on a small tag, a hang tag, a printed card or a gift box.

Wrist length is usually about 15–20 cm; longer lengths and 10 mm or 12 mm beads are available when you need them. You can also add bag charms and keychains or custom all-stone bracelets to the same order.

Before you order

Sweat, water and the two metals

A mixed metal bracelet is worn on the wrist, so it meets sweat, lotion and the odd splash. Natural stone should be kept away from sweat, pool water and long water exposure. We do not describe natural stone as waterproof or sweat-proof. Shops can tell customers to take the bracelet off before a workout, a swim or a shower.

Stainless steel clasps, spacers and findings are waterproof and tarnish-free. Gold-plated brass parts are rust-proof and waterproof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free. We mark the metal on the quotation and the sample so you know what you are selling.

Process

From idea to a packed shipment

  1. Send your idea

    Stones and colours, stainless steel or gold-plated brass, a reference photo if you have one, a rough quantity and your logo file if you have one is enough to start.

  2. Mockup and quote

    We reply as soon as possible with questions, a quotation and a mockup showing beads, metal parts, length, tag and packaging.

  3. Sample approval

    We make a sample and send photos and video with it. Check the stones, the metal and the fit before you approve it. Production starts after approval.

  4. Production

    Beads are graded by colour and size, then strung and finished with the agreed metal parts to the approved sample. Bulk work usually takes 15–30 days, depending on quantity and how much is customized.

  5. QC

    We check counts, lengths, knots, metal parts and packing, and can send inspection photos before dispatch.

  6. Consolidated shipping

    Bracelets, tags and boxes leave together. DHL or FedEx takes about 7–10 days; sea freight about 25–35 days for larger orders.

FAQ

Mixed metal bracelet questions

What is the minimum order for mixed metal beaded bracelets?

10 pieces per order in total. You can mix mixed-metal styles, all-stone bracelets, stones and colours inside those 10 pieces, and add stackable sets or beaded accessories to the same order. Branded extras such as engraved logo tags, printed cards or custom boxes can have their own minimums, and we confirm those in your quotation.

How much do mixed metal beaded bracelets cost?

Pricing depends on quantity, design and packaging, so we quote each inquiry. Tell us the stones, the metal finish, a rough quantity and whether you need logo tags or boxes, and the quotation will show every line clearly.

What metals do you use on a mixed metal beaded bracelet?

Two metals are standard with the natural-stone beads: stainless steel, and gold-plated brass. Stainless steel parts are waterproof and tarnish-free. Gold-plated brass parts are rust-proof and waterproof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free. We mark the metal on the quotation and the sample.

Do you make gold beaded bracelets for wholesale?

If you want a gold-look beaded bracelet for wholesale, this is usually a natural-stone bracelet with gold-plated brass spacers, accent pieces, clasps or findings — not a solid-gold piece. Send a reference photo or a short description of the look you want, and we will say what we can make.

Are mixed metal beaded bracelets waterproof or sweat-proof?

The natural stone is not. Natural stone should be kept away from sweat, pool water and long water exposure. We do not describe natural stone as waterproof or sweat-proof. Stainless steel parts are waterproof and tarnish-free. Gold-plated brass parts are rust-proof and waterproof, but we do not describe them as tarnish-free.

Can I mix these with all-stone bracelets in one order?

Yes. Mixing is normal for a first order. Many shops start with a small spread of mixed-metal and all-stone styles, see what sells, then reorder the ones that work. You can also add stackable sets, bag charms or keychains to the same shipment.

Can you add our logo or shop name?

Yes. Your logo or name can go on a small tag or charm on the bracelet, a hang tag, a printed card or a gift box. Artwork is reviewed with your mockup and sample before production.

Can I order a sample first?

Yes. After we agree a mockup, we can send a sample with photos and video. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project, and if any of the sample fee is credited against your bulk order, the quotation says so.

How long does a mixed metal bracelet order take?

Bulk production usually runs 15–30 days after you approve the sample, depending on quantity and how much is customized. Shipping is added on top: about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or about 25–35 days by sea freight.

Do I need separate suppliers for beads, metal parts and boxes?

No. We coordinate beads, metal parts, cord, tags, boxes, cards and packaging across our partner workshops in Ketang, Shanwei, run QC, and ship everything together. You deal with one team from quote to delivery.
