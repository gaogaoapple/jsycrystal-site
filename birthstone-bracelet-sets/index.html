Why shops stock them

A gift that answers "what should I buy her?"

A birthstone bracelet is an easy gift to choose. The customer only needs to know a birthday month. That makes a full 12-month row on the counter useful in the weeks before Christmas and Mother's Day, and for birthdays the rest of the year.

The catch for a small shop is stock. Twelve months times a reasonable depth per month gets expensive fast if a supplier wants 50 pieces per design. Our minimum is 10 pieces per order, counted across the whole order, so one bracelet per month is already a valid order. Most shops start with one or two per month, see which months sell in their area, and reorder those. If you later want the set to be yours alone, the same route covers custom bracelets and private label.

Process

How a birthstone set order runs

  1. Tell us what you need

    Your month list (or ours), bead size, rough quantity per month, cards or boxes, and delivery country.

  2. Quote

    We reply within 24 hours with questions, the stones we suggest for any hard months, and a quotation.

  3. Sample

    A sample set with photos and video, so you can check that the twelve colours sit well together. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project.

  4. Produce and check

    Beads are graded by colour and size before stringing. Each bracelet is checked against its month card before packing, and inspection photos can be sent before dispatch.

  5. Ship

    DHL or FedEx in about 7–10 days, or sea freight in about 25–35 days for larger orders. If the set is for a holiday season, count back from the date you want it on the shelf: sample, 15–30 days of production, then shipping.

FAQ

Birthstone bracelet set questions

What is the minimum order for birthstone bracelet sets?

10 pieces per order in total, mixed across months. One bracelet for each of the 12 months already meets the minimum. Branded month cards, tags or boxes can have their own minimums, and we confirm those in your quotation.

How much does a birthstone bracelet set cost?

Pricing depends on quantity, design and packaging, so we quote each inquiry. Tell us your month list, bead size, rough quantity and whether you need cards or boxes, and the quotation shows each line.

Which stones do you use for each month?

We start from the modern birthstone list and use a related natural stone for months whose gem is rarely made into round beads, for example clear quartz for April. You can also send your own list. Every stone is named as what it is on the quote and on the month card.

Is the turquoise real turquoise?

Much of the turquoise-coloured bead material on the market is dyed howlite or reconstituted stone. We state which material your December bracelet uses on the quote, and send photos of the batch before you order.

Is the citrine natural?

A lot of citrine sold in bead form is heat-treated amethyst. If a stone in your set is treated or dyed, we say so on the quote, so you can label it correctly in your shop.

Can you print month cards with my shop name?

Yes. A card per bracelet can carry the month, the stone name and your shop name or logo. You approve the wording and the design before printing.

Can I order a sample set first?

Yes. We can send a full sample set or just the months you want to check, with photos and video. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project, and if any of the sample fee is credited against your bulk order, the quotation says so.

How long does an order take?

Bulk production usually runs 15–30 days after you approve the sample. Shipping is added on top: about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or about 25–35 days by sea freight. For a holiday season, count back from the date you need the set on the shelf.

Can you supply other bead materials or crystal items?

Natural stone bracelets are our main line. If you need other bead materials or crystal items, tell us what you have in mind and we'll check what we can make or source, and say so plainly if it is not a good fit.
