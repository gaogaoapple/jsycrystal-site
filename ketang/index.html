Ketang: the market
behind the bracelets

JSY Crystal is based in Ketang Town, Lufeng, Shanwei, Guangdong — one of South China's major trading hubs for crystals and colored gemstones. This page explains what the hub is, and why being inside it changes what we can offer buyers.

What Ketang is

Ketang built its name the way trading towns always do: first the traders came for the goods, then the workshops came for the traders. Today the town's wholesale markets deal in crystals and colored gemstones across the full range — rough material, cut stones, beads and finished jewelry — and around them sits a dense cluster of cutting, polishing, drilling and stringing workshops, including the partner factory JSY represents exclusively overseas.

For a bracelet buyer, a hub like this works differently from a single distant factory. Material from many producing regions is on the ground in one place, prices are set by open competition between neighboring stalls and workshops, and a new stone type or bead size can usually be sampled in days rather than weeks.

What being based here does for your order

  • Category breadth: the full range of natural crystal and semi-precious stone bracelets is sourceable through the hub — our eight core stones are stocked lines, and the wider crystal range is available on request.
  • Full size range: every standard bead size is worked here daily, and custom sizes can be cut to order.
  • Fast, cheap sampling: materials and workshops within walking distance mean samples do not wait on internal shipping between cities.
  • Grading where it happens: we select and check material in the hub before production, and again before dispatch — quality is judged at the source of supply, not from photos.
  • Honest origins: Ketang is a trading and processing hub, not a mine. We will always tell you plainly what a stone is and how it was treated, rather than sell you an origin story.

Ketang questions

Where is Ketang, and why does it matter for crystal bracelets?

Ketang is a town in Lufeng, under Shanwei in eastern Guangdong, China. It has grown into one of South China's major wholesale hubs for crystals and colored gemstones, with trading markets and a dense cluster of cutting, polishing and bead workshops around them. Being based inside the hub means material choice, price comparison and sampling all happen within walking distance.

Are the stones mined in Ketang?

No — and any supplier who implies otherwise is not being straight with you. Ketang is a trading and processing hub, not a mining origin. Rough and finished stones arrive here from producing regions in China and abroad, and are traded, cut, graded and finished in the hub. What Ketang offers is selection and workmanship, not a mine story.

Can buyers visit Ketang and the factory?

Visits can be arranged — buyers who come to the hub can see the markets, review materials and samples in person, and inspect production with us. Contact us ahead of your trip and we will plan the visit around your sourcing list.

Which stones can be sourced through Ketang?

Effectively the full range of natural crystal and semi-precious stone bracelet types. Our eight core stones — amethyst, citrine, clear quartz, phantom quartz, gold rutilated quartz, golden obsidian, silver obsidian and rose quartz — are stocked lines, and the wider crystal range is available through the hub on request.
