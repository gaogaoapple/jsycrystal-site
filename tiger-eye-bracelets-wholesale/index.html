Why grading matters

What you get with a 7A tiger eye strand

Tiger eye varies a lot. Lower grades have dull beads, dark patches and hairline cracks mixed into the same strand, and that shows as soon as a customer turns the bracelet in the light.

7A is a trade grade, not a certificate. On our 7A strands the beads match in colour, each one shows a clear band of light that moves as the bracelet turns, and cracked or cloudy beads are taken out. Before you order, we send photos and video of the batch. After you approve a sample, the bulk order has to match it. That matters if you sell the same bracelet online for months and need the reorder to look like the listing photo.

Bead count is fixed by the bead size: about 22 beads at 8 mm, 18 at 10 mm and 16 at 12 mm. That gives one standard length per size. Buyers in the US often ask for a slightly larger fit, so tell us your customers' usual wrist size and we will check the length on the sample before bulk.

FAQ

Wholesale tiger eye bracelet questions

What does 7A mean on your tiger eye?

7A is a trade grade used in the Chinese bead market, not a lab certificate. On our 7A strands it means even colour from bead to bead, a clear moving band of light when you turn the bracelet, and few visible cracks or dull patches. We grade against the sample you approve, and the bulk order has to match that sample.

Which tiger eye colours do you offer?

Yellow (honey brown), golden, red and blue. Red and blue tiger eye are often heat-treated or dyed in the bead trade, so we state any treatment we know of on your quotation and send photos of the current batch before you order.

What bead sizes and lengths are available?

The 7A strands come in 8 mm (about 22 beads) and 10 mm (about 18 beads), with 12 mm (about 16 beads) on request. The stacking sets are 8 mm. Each bead size has one standard length on elastic cord. If your customers need a larger fit, tell us and we will check a longer strand on the sample.

Can I mix styles and colours in one order?

Yes. The minimum is 10 pieces per order, mixed across styles, colours and bead sizes, including men's strands, stacking sets and women's pieces in the same order. Branded boxes or tags can have their own minimums, which we confirm in the quotation.

Can the stacking sets be split?

Yes. The three strands in M-05, M-06 and M-07 are separate bracelets, so they can be sold as a set or one by one. You can also swap the tiger eye colour or the wood in a set.

Do you offer private-label gift boxes?

Yes. A set or a single bracelet can go into a gift box with your logo, and we can add a hang tag or a card with your wording. You approve the artwork before printing.

Are tiger eye bracelets waterproof?

No. Tiger eye, lava stone, obsidian and wood are natural materials, and the elastic cord ages faster with water, sweat and lotion. We suggest your customers take them off for swimming, showers and workouts. We print this care note on the card if you like.

How much do the tiger eye bracelets cost?

Pricing depends on quantity, design and packaging; we quote each inquiry. Send the style references (for example M-05 or T-39), rough quantities and your packaging, and the quote shows each line.

Who makes the bracelets?

JSY Crystal does not own a factory. We work with partner workshops and suppliers in China's natural stone bead trade, coordinated from our base in Ketang, Shanwei. We handle sourcing, sample approval, quality checks, packing and shipping, so you deal with one team.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. We can send samples of the styles you are considering, with photos and video before they ship. Sample fees and shipping are quoted per project, and the quotation states whether any of the sample fee is credited against your bulk order.

How long does an order take?

Bulk production usually runs 15–30 days after you approve the sample. Shipping is added on top: about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or about 25–35 days by sea freight.
