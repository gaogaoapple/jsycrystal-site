Corporate gifting questions

What quantities do corporate gift orders start from?

Our standard minimum is 10 pieces per order, and styles, stones and sizes can be mixed freely within that total. Custom components — gift boxes, printed cards and tags — have their own minimums, which are confirmed in your quotation before you commit.

How long does a corporate gift order take?

Standard bulk production runs 15–30 days depending on quantity and customization, plus shipping: about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or 25–35 days by sea freight. Tell us your event date and we will confirm a realistic schedule — rush delivery is never assumed.

Can we add our company branding?

Yes. Printed message cards with your wording, gift boxes and pouches, and private-label pendants or tags are all available. Artwork is reviewed and confirmed with your sample before production.

Can we see a sample before the full order?

Yes. Samples are charged at cost, the buyer covers shipping, and the sample fee can be deducted from a first order that reaches the qualifying quantity.

Which stones work best for corporate gifts?

Amethyst, citrine and rose quartz are the most-gifted stones in our range; smoky tones and obsidian suit more understated programs. We will suggest a stone and bead size mix for your audience and budget — 8mm is the safest all-round choice.

Do you ship to multiple countries?

We ship worldwide via DHL, FedEx or sea freight. Consolidated delivery to one office or event venue is the most common arrangement for gift programs.
