See it before it is made

Your logo on a mockup first, then the sample

Corporate buyers usually have to show the gift to someone else before they can sign off — a manager, a client team, an event committee. So we start with a picture, not a production run.

  1. Send your logo and wording. A vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) is best; a clear PNG works for a first look. Add the card message and any event name or year.
  2. We send a mockup. It shows your logo on the tag, card or gift box with the stone and bead size we suggest, so your team can compare options side by side.
  3. You approve or change it. Swap the stone, move the logo from the tag to the box, shorten the card text — changes at this stage cost nothing in production time.
  4. The physical sample follows the approved mockup. Sample fees, shipping and timing are agreed before the sample is made, and bulk production starts only after you approve the sample.

Small logo tags have little room: a short company name or a simple mark reads better than a full logo with a tagline. If the tag is too small for your artwork, we will suggest putting the full logo on the card or box instead.

Production and shipping are separate clocks. These are the timings we work to; your quotation confirms the schedule for your quantity and packaging.

A worked example: gifts needed at a sales kickoff on a fixed date, shipped by express. Count back up to 10 days for shipping, up to 30 days for production, then the days your team needs to approve the mockup and the sample, plus a few days of buffer for inspection and customs. If the sample still has to be approved by a committee, start earlier.

Import duties and taxes in your country are set by your customs authority; your broker or forwarder can confirm them for your shipment.

Year-round ordering

Plan around your own gifting calendar

Corporate bracelets work all year — client appreciation, onboarding kits, company anniversaries, events, Lunar New Year and other seasons. Work backwards from the day the gifts are handed out, and allow time for the mockup and sample plus production and shipping.

  • Allow sample time plus production time. Use the timeline above and add your own internal approval time. Standard bulk production runs 15–30 days after sample approval, plus about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or 25–35 days by sea freight. If the date is close, a stock stone with a printed card and box is quicker than an engraved tag.
  • Seasonal pauses. Workshops in China slow down and close for a period around Lunar New Year, so January–February events and door gifts in Asian markets need that buffer built in. Tell us the date you need the goods and we will confirm a realistic schedule — rush delivery is never assumed.

Corporate gifting questions

What quantities do corporate gift orders start from?

Our standard minimum is 10 pieces per order, and styles, stones and sizes can be mixed freely within that total. Custom components — gift boxes, printed cards and tags — have their own minimums, which are confirmed in your quotation before you commit.

Can we see our logo on the gift before a sample is made?

Yes. Send your logo file and card wording, and we send back a mockup showing it on the tag, card or box. You approve or change the layout first; the physical sample is made from the approved mockup.

How long does a corporate gift order take?

Standard bulk production runs 15–30 days after sample approval, depending on quantity and customization, plus shipping: about 7–10 days by DHL or FedEx, or 25–35 days by sea freight. Tell us your event date and we will confirm a realistic schedule — rush delivery is never assumed.

When should we order for a gift program or event?

Work backwards from the day the gifts are handed out: allow time for the mockup and sample, up to 30 days of production, up to 10 days by express or 35 by sea, and a buffer for inspection and customs. Plan around your own gifting calendar — client appreciation, onboarding kits, company anniversaries, events, Lunar New Year and other seasons. Workshops in China slow down and close for a period around Lunar New Year, so build that in if your date falls near it.

How is pricing worked out?

Pricing depends on quantity, design and packaging; we quote each inquiry. Send the recipient count, stone preference, branding and packaging you have in mind, and the quote lists each item separately.

Can we add our company branding?

Yes. Printed message cards with your wording, gift boxes and pouches, and private-label pendants or tags are all available. Artwork is reviewed on a mockup and confirmed with your sample before production.

Can we see a sample before the full order?

Yes. Samples are charged at cost, the buyer covers shipping, and the sample fee can be deducted from a first order that reaches the qualifying quantity.

Which stones work best for corporate gifts?

Amethyst, citrine and rose quartz are the most-gifted stones in our range; smoky tones and obsidian suit more understated programs. We will suggest a stone and bead size mix for your audience and budget — 8mm is the safest all-round choice.

Do you ship to multiple countries?

We ship worldwide via DHL, FedEx or sea freight. Consolidated delivery to one office or event venue is the most common arrangement for gift programs.

Do I need separate suppliers for boxes, tags and beads?

No. We coordinate beads, charms, tags, boxes, cards and packaging across our partner workshops in Shanwei, run QC, and ship everything together. You deal with one team from quote to delivery.
